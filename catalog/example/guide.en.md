# Example airdrop (template)

> Template for protocol guides. Copy this folder, then replace every section. Real guides are
> written in plain English, checked against the official app, and never machine-translated.

## What it is

One or two sentences: what the protocol did, and why funds can still be sitting in a wallet.

## Who is eligible

Who can claim, and how the check decides it (which contract, which network).

## How to get it out

1. Open the official app: https://example.com/claim (link verified on the official domain).
2. Connect the wallet that holds the balance, inside the official app only.
3. Claim and confirm the transaction.

## What it costs

The estimated network fee today is shown in the report. Say here what to expect and when it is not
worth it.

## Deadline

The last day to claim, or "no deadline".
